# Relation to the GDPR

The GDPR and the CRA have different addressees:

| | GDPR | CRA |
|---|---|---|
| Who is responsible | Each solidarity farm, as controller of its members' data | The manufacturer of Tapir (see [Scope](scope.md)) |
| Role of FoodCoopX | Processor when hosting, with a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) | Manufacturer, if in scope |
| Subject | Lawful processing of personal data | Security of the product |

## Where they overlap

Among its essential requirements, the CRA asks a product to process only the data it needs, to
protect its confidentiality, and to let users delete their data securely and permanently. These
are the same topics the GDPR covers from the controller's side:

| Topic | GDPR | CRA |
|---|---|---|
| Data minimisation | Art. 5(1)(c) | Annex I, Part I |
| Deletion | Art. 17, with exceptions for retention duties | Annex I, Part I |
| Security of processing | Art. 32 | Annex I, Part I |
| Breach or incident | Notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours (Art. 33) | Report severe incidents to the authorities (Art. 14) |

## Deletion (#1123)

Issue #1123 asks for deletion that follows the GDPR: delete on request, but keep what
cooperative, commercial and tax law require. Under the GDPR this is a duty of each solidarity
farm, which depends on Tapir to fulfil it. Under the CRA, secure deletion would also be a
requirement for Tapir itself. A solution for #1123 therefore serves both.

**Open:** which data has to be kept for how long. This is for each cooperative to confirm with
its auditing association or data protection officer.
